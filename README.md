## 🎯 About Me

I'm an **AI/ML Engineer** who is energized by hard, ambiguous problems — the kind that don't have a clean textbook answer. I hold an M.Sc in Artificial Intelligence & ML Ops (NLP specialization) and am currently completing an M.Tech in Data Science, because I set a high bar for my own expertise and keep raising it.

🏗️ I design and ship **production-grade GenAI and LLM systems**: multi-agent research agents, retrieval-augmented pipelines, and MLOps infrastructure that convert messy, unstructured data into decisions people can actually act on.

💼 Currently a **Data Scientist at InSync Analytics**, building LLM-powered pipelines (Google Gemini, LangChain, LangGraph) that extract and structure financial data from regulatory filings at scale — work that demands both engineering rigor and business judgment.

🧭 Previously at **IIT Bombay's NCAIR**, deploying Hugging Face models for NLP and building computer vision systems from the ground up.

📈 Every project below represents a deliberate attempt to take on something harder than the last — I'd rather stretch and learn than stay comfortable.

🌍 Based in **Mumbai, India** 🇮🇳
